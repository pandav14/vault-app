Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about Vault: Hide Photos, Passwords? We have answers.

How does the Calculator Stealth mode work?

When Calculator Stealth is enabled, Vault replaces its launcher icon and UI with a standard working calculator. Performing calculations works normally. To open your vault, simply enter your custom mathematical PIN sequence into the calculator and tap the '=' key.

Is any of my photo or password data uploaded to the cloud?

No. Vault operates on a zero-knowledge, local-only architecture. Zero kilobytes of data leave your device. All databases are encrypted with AES-256 and stored locally in protected Android app storage.

What is a Decoy Profile?

A Decoy Profile provides plausible deniability under coercion. You can set up a secondary "fake" password. Entering this password unlocks an entirely separate, harmless vault profile with benign photos or notes, hiding your true personal vault data.

How does the On-Device Local AI (BYOM) work?

Vault includes a Built-in Local AI Engine powered by MediaPipe LLM Inference. You can select lightweight models (like Gemma 2B or Phi-2) saved on your device storage. The AI runs completely on your phone's processor without needing internet or cloud API keys.

How can I transfer or backup my encrypted data?

You can generate password-protected `.vault` backup files at any time and save them to your internal storage, USB drive, or personal cloud storage. To restore on a new device, simply install Vault, select your `.vault` backup file, and enter your backup password.
