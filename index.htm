Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about Vault? Here are answers directly from the official guide.

How does Calculator Stealth mode work?

In Settings > Security > Stealth Mode, turning on disguise replaces Vault's launcher icon and UI with a standard working calculator. To unlock, simply type your secret PIN sequence (e.g. 123+456) on the calculator keypad and press '='.

What is a Decoy Vault and how does it protect me?

Enabled in Settings > Security > Decoy Vault, it creates a secondary fake vault with its own master password. If forced to unlock your phone, entering your decoy password displays harmless dummy records while leaving your true personal vault completely hidden.

Is any of my photo or password data uploaded to servers?

No. Vault has zero internet permissions. All photos, videos, passwords, and media are encrypted with AES-256 via SQLite3MC and stored strictly inside protected local device storage.

How does On-Device AI (BYOM) run without internet?

In Settings > AI & Automation, you can select offline converted model files (`.bin` or `.litertlm` like Gemma 2B or Phi-2) for MediaPipe LLM Inference. All model execution occurs in phone RAM without transmitting data online.

How do encrypted `.vault` backups work?

In Settings > Data Management > Backup Vault, you can generate AES-256 encrypted `.vault` backup files protected by a backup password. Restoring lets you choose 'Merge' to combine records or 'Replace' for fresh device migrations.

What happens when I delete photos or passwords?

Deleted items move to Recycle Bin (Trash) in Settings > Data Management. You can restore items anytime or configure Auto-Clean to automatically purge items after 1 to 90 days.
